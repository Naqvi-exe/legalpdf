Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

The core principle: LegalPDF processes all documents locally in your web browser. Your files are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers.

1. Document Processing

Every PDF tool on LegalPDF operates using client-side JavaScript (via libraries like pdf-lib and PDF.js). When you select a file, it is loaded directly into your browser's memory. The processing (merging, splitting, redacting) occurs on your device. The resulting file is downloaded directly back to your device. At no point is the file content transmitted over the internet to us or any third party.

2. Information We Collect

Because we do not have user accounts, we do not collect names, passwords, or document data. If you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content solely to respond to your inquiry. This data is processed via Formspree, a third-party form provider.

3. Cookies and Analytics

LegalPDF does not use tracking cookies for advertising. If basic analytics are enabled (e.g., page views), they are privacy-focused and do not track individual user behavior across the web.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third parties: Formspree (to route contact form emails) and Google Fonts (for typography). Please review their respective privacy policies.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at saan63238@gmail.com.
